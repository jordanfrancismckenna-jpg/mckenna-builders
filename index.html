Our story

The McKenna name has been on building sites across south east Essex for over forty years.

What started in 1985 is now a father-and-sons business, and the trade has been handed down along with the attention to detail. Our speciality is ornamental brickwork and stonework, the kind of finish that makes a house stand out on the street. We're just as at home building a complete property from bare ground.

Being a family firm means the people who quote for your job are the people who build it. Customers tell us they value being kept up to date, so we make a point of it from the first visit to the final handover.

We're always happy for anyone thinking of using us to see our finished work and to speak to people we've built for.
